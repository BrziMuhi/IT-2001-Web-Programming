How can I reserve a table?

Visit our Reservations page and enter your preferred date, time and number of guests. Online reservation processing is currently under development.

Where can I see your menu?

You can explore our food selection and prices on the Menu page. We offer starters, main courses and desserts.

Can I make a reservation for a group?

Our reservation form allows you to specify the number of guests. For special arrangements, please contact the restaurant.

Can I request special dietary arrangements?

You can include dietary preferences or other requests in the Special Requests field on the reservation form. Availability of accommodations must be confirmed by the restaurant.

How can I contact Verdant?

Visit our Contact page to find restaurant information and our contact form.

Still Have Questions?

We're happy to help with questions about our menu, reservations or special occasions. Get in touch with Verdant Restaurant.
